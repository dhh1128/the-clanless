# L1 — Arcs and dilemmas

Planning layer L1 (`plan/layers.md`). For each principal: where they stand at the end of Cordimancy, where The Clanless leaves them, how far that is, the conscious dilemma and the confident blind spot, and the rungs between. L1 owns what a break or a turn *means*; L5 owns the count of what is held down before it; L2 owns the labels and names.

This is the **canon half** (ruling, Q-PR4D). The content of each dilemma and blind spot lives in the character's truth profile (`plan/truth-profiles/`), and this file points there rather than restating it. The rungs are proposals `[?]`: they give the arc its shape for the outline to build on, and they say nothing yet about which scene carries each rung. Citations are to `~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md` at cordimancy commit `64b2f69`.

## How far each one moves

The brief names a failure: *"too much or too little movement from the emotional posture/maturity of characters at the end of Cordimancy."* Two limits set the range:
- **Not too little.** Each principal ends The Clanless having chosen something that costs, and holding it (`plan/brief.md`, *"the overcoming that they do has to be real"*).
- **Not too much.** Book 3 still has to happen. There, *"Kinora comes into her own as a young adult,"* *"Malena and Toril's marriage is mature,"* and Oji dies defending the people he loves (`plan/sequels.md`). So in The Clanless the marriage becomes a love they choose, not yet a mature one. Kinora comes to own herself as a child, not yet as a young adult. Oji learns to let himself be loved, but he doesn't yet die for it.

## Toril

- **Starts** (`plan/truth-profiles/toril.md`, "Where he stands"): stoneless, semi-mute from the brass, blamed for Noemi, hiding the full cost of the unwinding (`:4537`–`4542`, `:4706`). His vow: *"I'll still father those kids"* (`:4542`).
- **Ends** (`plan/brief.md`): *"forced to confront the cost of his idealistic choices, and groaning under the weight, but ultimately shouldering it with a will and finding joy and peace on the other side."*
- **Dilemma:** the clan, or the children. It is decided by the escalated staff trade with Rovin (ruling, Q-QC4N; placement open).
- **Blind spot:** the orphans as his to keep. He is certain he protects them, and doesn't see that part of what he protects is his own need. Proposal.
- **Rungs** `[?]`:
  1. He is mute and stoneless, and the clan calls him traitor. He holds his name in silence, and the silence is read as consent.
  2. Malena tells him about the child. He says he accepts it, before he means it all the way (`plan/truth-profiles/toril.md`, "The child").
  3. The indenture. His name, *Seeker of the Helpless*, is tested against a child he bought for his kitchen. He pays with a public confession and the word *daughter* (ruling, Q-QC4N), and his blind spot is answered: he frees her before asking her to stay.
  4. The staff goes in a trade for everyone's freedom. Clan or children is decided.
  5. He holds the infant, and the acceptance he spoke at rung 2 has become true.

## Malena

- **Starts** (`plan/truth-profiles/malena.md`, "Where she stands"): suspects she is pregnant, and not by Toril (`:4122`, `:2431`). She has declared herself dead to him (`:1083`). She has thrown away his heartstone (`:4077`) and then hugged him back (`:4686`–`4701`). In her line, childbirth kills women (`:224`). At the very end she asks whether it's *"too late to accept 'labor' and 'heart'"* (`:4519`).
- **Ends** (`plan/brief.md`): she discovers that the name she once glimpsed *"is perhaps far more profound and desirable than she once credited,"* and *"that there can be joy after and even inside the pain."*
- **Dilemma:** whether, and when, to tell Toril. Daniel's words.
- **Blind spot:** Tupa. *"More confident and less self-doubting (and perhaps, more mistaken?)"*. Daniel's words, with a question mark of his own.
- **Rungs** `[?]`:
  1. She hides the pregnancy, and every day of delay is a lie of silence she calls kindness.
  2. She tells Toril.
  3. She is caught out over Kinora, and pays with her share of her father's estate through Veshumi (ruling, Q-C9KB).
  4. Labor and birth: Shivi's *"the joy that mother feels when the new labor is over"* (`:2317`) comes true in her own body (L16).
  5. She holds *labor* and *heart* as her name.

## Oji

- **Starts** (`plan/truth-profiles/oji.md`): thirteen, an adult, unconscious after the last aiki trance (`:4704`). He doesn't know whether he killed Luim (`plan/backstory.md` OJ1). He has kept his own story to himself (OJ3).
- **Ends** (`plan/brief.md`): he *"finds a brother and a sister of the heart, and … this heals a hollow place in his heart."*
- **Dilemma:** his starving people in the south, or his chosen family going north, paid for in days of his own life. Proposal.
- **Blind spot:** belonging has to be earned by dying for it. Proposal. Book 3's death is right, so what The Clanless changes is *why* he would die (`plan/truth-profiles/oji.md`, "Where his arc goes").
- **Rungs** `[?]`:
  1. He wakes. The refugees call him *parricide*, a charge he can't confirm or refute.
  2. He burns days of life in aiki for the band, and is welcomed for it. This is the danger L3 flags: belonging that reads as wages.
  3. Someone refuses his sacrifice, or tends him when he is useless, and he is loved anyway.
  4. The brotherhood with Toril is confirmed out loud, in this book or in book 3 (`plan/sequels.md`).

## Kinora

- **Starts** (`plan/truth-profiles/kinora-and-tupa.md`): eight or nine, hired help, grieving Maco, Elesel and Cook, and guilty about being glad her stepfather is gone. Her first question at the palace is about Cricket (`:4678`). She already trusts Toril (`:3530`).
- **Ends** `[?]`: she owns herself, and is chosen as a daughter after she chooses. The book-3 self-name is planted (`plan/sequels.md`: *"Kinora comes into her own as a young adult"* belongs to book 3).
- **Dilemma:** the indenture. She consents to go, to spare them (ruling, Q-YKE6, on the claimants). Its direction is Daniel's; the profile marks the dilemma itself `[?]`.
- **Blind spot:** her place in this family is a job (ruling, Q-GAPV).
- **Rungs** `[?]`:
  1. Cricket returns and is formally the couple's child. She is glad and jealous at once.
  2. The baby is born into the family by blood and by law. She is still hired help.
  3. The indenture. She consents to go, and does it the way she left Cricket in the rushes.
  4. They break the indenture first, then ask her. She chooses, and only then is *daughter* said (ruling, Q-QC4N).

## Tupa

- **Starts** (`plan/truth-profiles/kinora-and-tupa.md`): eleven. Shivi died in her place (`:4734`), and she delivered the acorn into Toril's hands (`:4751`). Veshumi speaks for their dead parents, and the fosterage at Erim's still waits.
- **Ends:** at her naming day she chooses Malena and the clanless, and walking out of the river makes both sisters clanless (ruling, Q-M93X). Her self-name is open.
- **Dilemma:** Father's wishes, or Malena (ruling, Q-P7VW).
- **Blind spot:** her father loved her well (ruling, Q-P7VW). What she carries is Shivi's death, and it must not become Oji's blind spot as well (`plan/truth-profiles/kinora-and-tupa.md`).
- **Rungs** `[?]`:
  1. The debt to Shivi is a warm coal she carries.
  2. She sees Kinora more clearly than the adults do, and tells Malena that Kinora has consented and is leaving.
  3. Her naming day in Sotalio, under Rovin's priest, with Veshumi on the bank (ruling, Q-M93X).
