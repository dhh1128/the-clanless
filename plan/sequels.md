# Sequels: what The Clanless must set up

Daniel's statements about the book after this one. The Clanless doesn't show any of this, but it has to stay consistent with all of it, and some of it it must plant.

## Book 3

- **Working title "Blood Rift"**, subject to revision.
- **The Blood Rift is inhabited by the clanless, and redeemed.**
- **Lin's arc with the seedling is completed.** The seedling overcomes the barrenness at the same time as the clanless clan begins to flourish. (The canon is Lin's Seedling at the heart of the Rift, and the legend of the twin queens Lin and Viro, `~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md:3861`–`3990`.)
- **Kinora comes into her own as a young adult.**
- **Malena and Toril's marriage is mature.**
- **Oji dies** in a dramatic scene, giving his life to defend the people he loves from Gorumim's advancing soldiers. Malena holds him as he dies and weeps for him. *"This is the completion of the cycle we're heading towards."*
- **Gorumim is alive and commanding soldiers in book 3.** So whatever The Clanless shows of him has to leave him alive.

## Set up in The Clanless, or confirmed in book 3

- **The brotherhood of Toril and Oji is confirmed out loud**, possibly formally, in a blood-brother rite, either in this book or in book 3. The rite would need to be the world's own, not a borrowed one.

More to come from Daniel.
