# Truth profile: Kael

Each claim cites `~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md` (cordimancy commit `64b2f69`), or quotes Daniel, or is marked `[?]` as the session's proposal.

## Daniel's statement

*"Kael is present to show that Osipi are not uniform in their support for Luim's agenda, and to perhaps begin to broaden people's conceptions beyond what Oji is like."*

## Canon he stands on

- The ahu are the osipi who wear the ivona, the battle collar, and are far more dangerous than ordinary osipi warriors (`:789`). An ivona *"holds tales"* (`:654`).
- Eighteen ahu and one aiki were brought in chains to the war council at Bakar (`:774`, `:1306`). The ahu served Luim, the cacique, and through him Gorumim's bond with the osipi (`~/code/me/sivanea.com/cordimancy/bonus-prologue.md`).
- Ahu jeered at Oji: *"Cowardice is being afraid to follow your cacique"* (`:3118`). That is the ahu creed Kael has to break with.
- Oji's fused compound verbs are his own, not a trait of his people (`plan/truth-profiles/oji.md`). The ahu in canon speak plainly.

## How he differs from Oji `[?]`

- **He belongs to a family.** Oji has no clan and envies anyone who gets to choose an allegiance (`:2380`). Kael has elders, a mate or children, and a place among his own people. His break with Luim is a choice of kin over his cacique, not of friends over a people who never wanted him.
- **His ivona is full.** Oji's was empty (`plan/backstory.md` OJ3). Kael earned every tale on his collar under Luim, so breaking with Luim costs him his honors as well as his loyalty.
- **He is slow to speak, by osipi standards,** and he is suspicious, where Oji is fast and delighted.

## Two kinds of struggle `[?]`

- **The conscious dilemma: his honor, or his conscience.** His ivona records deeds done at Luim's command. Some of them he is now ashamed of, and the collar is still his. Taking it off or keeping it is a choice he feels in his body.
- **The confident blind spot: humans are all the same.** He is the mirror of every Kelun villager who calls Oji *yolk-sucker* (`:2057`). He helps Toril's people while sure they will turn on the osipi the moment it suits them. That widens the book's picture from both sides: osipi aren't uniform, and prejudice doesn't flow in only one direction. Over the book a few specific humans become exceptions to him, and he can't fold them back into the rule.

## How he bends truth `[?]`

By silence about what he did for Luim. He doesn't lie about it. He just never brings it up, until someone he has come to respect asks him straight out.
