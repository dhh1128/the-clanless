# Truth profile: Corim

Each claim cites `~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md` (cordimancy commit `64b2f69`), or quotes Daniel, or is marked `[?]` as the session's proposal.

## Canon

- He lives in Two Forks. He verifies the manifests of the ingot barges from Kelun's mines and sends shipments across Zufa, and he does metalwork on the side (`:2931`, `:2937`–`2938`). *"I have safer places to keep the bulk of the money"* (`:2938`).
- He has known the house of Hasha a long time: *"That's the most beauty I've seen from the house of Hasha since your mother was young, Toril."* (`:2932`). He is cheerful, and his welcome eases them both (`:2930`).
- He has twin boarhounds: *"Pay no attention to my bodyguard. He's harmless to friends. And so is his twin, asleep in the back room."* (`:2934`–`2936`).
- He took the risk of walking his cart back to draw off Gorumim's scouts (`:3405`), and he agreed to care for Cricket (`:3409`).
- *"Corim liked him."* (`:3628`)

## Daniel's statement

He is in his mid-forties and single, like his sister Desta. He goes into exile because of *"friendship with Toril, loyalty to Toril as the true head of his clan, distrust of Rovin, and a genuine liking for the kids. He has many motives, all good, and his ties to his old home are mostly about comfort. He can live without that."*

## The hounds

Daniel: *"perhaps Desta brings them with her?"* `[?]` Corim goes ahead to Toril, and Desta follows from Two Forks with Cricket, Hika and the twin boarhounds. A childless woman of forty-five arrives at the exiles' door with a toddler and three large dogs, and the girls will love it.

## Toril's wealth

**Canon.** Corim is the family's agent in Two Forks, managing *"shipments we send down from the mines"*, and he keeps *"a small treasury for us"* (`~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md:2901`). The strongbox is *"full to the brim"* and *"mostly gold"*, while Malena's father, *"the wealthiest man in Abizaro"*, had a box of silver with a few crowns in it (`:2968`). Toril took a hundred crowns in a money belt, and said: *"I have money in other places, but I may not be able to get to it if Rovin gets his way."* (`:2972`–`2981`).

**Daniel.** *"Toril is quite wealthy; Hasha was a careful steward and saver, and did not live extravagantly. The money Corim is managing belongs to Toril. … it's not pennilessness, in the absolute and permanent sense, that faces Toril or any he's connected with. He could buy out Rovin, easily enough. His wealth dwarfs what Malena can bring to bear with her inheritance. We need to work out some kind of scenario that makes this wealth and its potential unavailability/timing problems work to sharpen the story."*

**The scenario (ruling, Q-C9KB).**
1. **Rovin freezes Hasha's estate, and canon hands him the pretext.** He already claims Toril used magic to force Hasha's story of the succession (`:1157`). If the succession was coerced, then who owns Hasha's estate is in question. Rovin holds it *"for the clan"* until the clan decides who its chief is. He believes he is keeping a madman from squandering the clan's mines. That makes him trapped, not evil.
2. **The indenture falls inside the freeze.** The richest man in Kelun can't pay for one girl's freedom in Sotalio. The hundred crowns went on the rescue. The only coin within reach is Malena's share of her father's estate, and it comes through Veshumi, whose husband serves the man who froze Toril's. Releasing it is Veshumi's choice against her husband's patron, and it sharpens her dilemma, her sisters or her place (`plan/truth-profiles/veshumi.md`). The amount is small. What it costs is who has to give it.
3. **The *clanless* sentence takes the fortune.** A clanless man holds nothing under clan law. In the escalated trade, the staff goes and Hasha's estate inside Kelun goes with it: the mines, the shipments, the ledgers. This is the weight Toril groans under (`plan/brief.md`). He gives away his father's careful life's work for people Rovin calls nobody.
4. **What lies outside Kelun is real but far away.** The Two Forks box and Corim's *"safer places"* (`:2938`) may sit outside Kelun's law, so the clanless are not paupers. But that money is downriver, it's winter, and Gorumim has friends in Two Forks (`:3333`). Desta brings what she can, with the boarhounds Corim calls *"part of my insurance"* (`:2938`), and the road is where she could be robbed. The fortune that stays out of reach becomes what the Rift settlement is built on in book 3 (`plan/sequels.md`).

**Open:** whether Two Forks lies under Kelun's law. Canon puts Kelun's ingots passing through it (`:2937`) but doesn't say whose town it is.
