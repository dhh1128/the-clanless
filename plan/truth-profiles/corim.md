# Truth profile: Corim

Each claim cites `~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md` (cordimancy commit `64b2f69`), or quotes Daniel, or is marked `[?]` as the session's proposal.

## Canon

- He lives in Two Forks. He verifies the manifests of the ingot barges from Kelun's mines and sends shipments across Zufa, and he does metalwork on the side (`:2931`, `:2937`–`2938`). *"I have safer places to keep the bulk of the money"* (`:2938`).
- He has known the house of Hasha a long time: *"That's the most beauty I've seen from the house of Hasha since your mother was young, Toril."* (`:2932`). He is cheerful, and his welcome eases them both (`:2930`).
- He has twin boarhounds: *"Pay no attention to my bodyguard. He's harmless to friends. And so is his twin, asleep in the back room."* (`:2934`–`2936`).
- He took the risk of walking his cart back to draw off Gorumim's scouts (`:3405`), and he agreed to care for Cricket (`:3409`).
- *"Corim liked him."* (`:3628`)

## Daniel's statement

He is in his mid-forties and single, like his sister Desta. He goes into exile because of *"friendship with Toril, loyalty to Toril as the true head of his clan, distrust of Rovin, and a genuine liking for the kids. He has many motives, all good, and his ties to his old home are mostly about comfort. He can live without that."*

## The hounds

Daniel: *"perhaps Desta brings them with her?"* `[?]` Corim goes ahead to Toril, and Desta follows from Two Forks with Cricket, Hika and the twin boarhounds. A childless woman of forty-five arrives at the exiles' door with a toddler and three large dogs, and the girls will love it.

## Hazard `[?]`

Corim has money and *"safer places"* for it. The Kinora indenture is meant to cost Malena her share of her father's estate (`plan/truth-profiles/kinora-and-tupa.md`). If Corim can simply pay, the cost goes away. He must not offer, or must be refused, or his money must be out of reach under Rovin. Toril already worried that he *"may not be able to get to it if Rovin gets his way"* (`:2981`).
