# Truth profile: Toril

Each claim is marked *canon*, citing a line of `~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md` (cordimancy commit `64b2f69`), or *proposal*, which waits for his correction. The arc comes from `plan/brief.md`: Toril is *"forced to confront the cost of his idealistic choices, and groaning under the weight, but ultimately shouldering it with a will and finding joy and peace on the other side."*

## Where he stands when The Clanless opens (canon)

- **His name.** On his naming day he took *"Helper…"* among the stones, and Gitám later gave the name back to him as *"Seeker of the Helpless"* / *"Helpless Seeker"* (`:189`–`193`). The name comes from Gitám, the Unpaired One, *"god of dregs, lost causes, broken pottery, and orphans"* (`:193`). In the ordeal Gitám asks: *"Will you hold your name—be who you've chosen—even when all hope is lost?"* (`:1565`) and *"Never forget the 'helpless' in the name you chose. Who is helpless?"* He answers *"Malena,"* and *"Silence told Toril that his answer was inadequate."* (`:1716`–`1718`)
- **What he gave up.** He traded his lip magic to begin the ordeal, then *"Unwound magic from my… own bones. Sata fork, after all."* He is *"Stoneless now!"* (`:4537`–`4542`).
- **His vow, made under torture, which Malena overheard:** *"I'll still father those kids, unmake… your orphans. Can't stop me."* (`:4542`)
- **His mouth.** Gorumim jammed the smoking brass of the staff *"between screaming teeth"* while speaking an incantation. The damage was to *"his tongue, his throat, his jaws, his lips"* (`:4543`, `:4552`). His fingers were broken on one hand (`:4468`–`4482`). By the novel's last scene he is *"semi-mute with pain"* and speaks in short phrases, pausing *"to prepare damaged lips"* (`:4706`, `:4714`). How much speech returns, and how fast, is *open*. The Gemini line *"husky voice gradually returns"* is not canon.
- **He hides the full cost.** Malena learns of the stoneless bargain only by overhearing it: *"When Toril had confessed his trading away of magic, he'd said nothing about* this *bargain."* (`:4538`)
- **Wit under the worst pain.** Under torture, *"a queer, broken, wet sort of chuckle … unmistakably laughter"*, and then *"Two lips. Two liars."* (`:4531`–`4532`)
- **The blame he carries.** Malena *"sensed that the destruction of Noemi and her own misery were connected"* to his defiance at the war council (`:1594`). The clan calls him coward and traitor: *"The clan already… hates me"* (`:4532`).

## Label and name

Daniel's essay on lying (`~/code/me/sivanea.com/on-lying-and-truth-telling.md`) says labels lie: they are tidy buckets someone applies to a messy soul. The ordeal says names are true: *"With people, all true names are self-names."* (`:1445`). The Clanless can run on that difference. Others *apply* labels to Toril: *stoneless*, *traitor*, *coward*, *cripple*, and finally Rovin's *clanless*. Toril *chose* his name, and the book is about whether he holds it. The title inverts when the exiles take the label Rovin imposed and hold it as a name. This applies to every character, not only Toril.

## Two kinds of struggle

- **The conscious dilemma: the clan, or the children.** He is the legitimate chief of Kelun. Hasha's legacy and the clan's survival under Rovin's growing war are his to defend. Contesting Rovin may split the clan and put the orphans in the middle of it. Yielding abandons his people to a war he knows is founded on a lie. He feels both goods fully. In Sotalio, at the gate, he lets the staff go while holding Paka. That is the moment he chooses, and it costs him the clan.
- **The confident blind spot: the orphans as his to keep.** He vowed to *"father those kids."* He is stoneless, so these children are the only fatherhood left to him. When a relative comes to claim an orphan, he is certain he is protecting the child. The reader may see that some of what he is protecting is his own need. His love is real, and so is his possessiveness, and he can't see the second.

## The child (proposal)

When Malena tells him, he says he accepts the child, and his doubts are still alive. By the essay's account, that sentence is either a lie or *"The Truth beside which all other truths pale"*: the mother at the hospital who says *"I love you"* and not *"I'm angry."* By the ordeal's account, it becomes true if he holds it: *"the ordeal is to continue to choose the name when you understand its cost."* So his acceptance is a self-name spoken before he has earned it, and the book's job is to let him earn it, up to the moment he holds the infant. His vow at `:4542` already set this up: he swore to father children who are not his before he knew there was one.

## How he bends truth (canon and proposal)

- **Partial truth about his own cost** (canon, `:4538`). He tells the part that spares the other person, and some of what he spares is his own pride. The essay counts this kind of omission as lying.
- **Restraint read as consent** (proposal). Now that he is mute, every silence of his gets read as agreement, by Rovin, by the clan and by Malena. The essay's dilemma of silence that lies becomes his daily condition.

## How he tells truth (canon)

- To power, directly. He was the one dissenting vote at the war council.
- Through gallows wit, even while being tortured.

## What the reader should like (proposal, for REGARD)

He laughs at his torturer. He gives his last millet cakes to a blind beggar (`:2909`–`2917`). He keeps getting up. And he is honest about wanting things, including Malena's love and his staff, which keeps him from becoming a saint.
