# L2 — Labels and names

Planning layer L2 (`plan/layers.md`). For each character it records each label pinned on them, by whom and where; the name they hold against it; and the rung they're on, up to the exiles taking *clanless* as a name. After that rung, it records each scene where the outside world's use of the label presses on someone, so that the inversion is enacted and not declared once.

The governing lines: *"Labels are imposed; names are chosen and held"* (`plan/brief.md`), and *"With people, all true names are self-names"* (`~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md:1445`). Daniel's essay says labels lie because they are tidy buckets applied to a messy soul (`~/code/me/sivanea.com/on-lying-and-truth-telling.md`).

This is the **canon half** (ruling, Q-PR4D). Citations are to `cordimancy.md` at cordimancy commit `64b2f69`. A label is marked true, half-true or false as the text plays it out. Placement waits for the scene list.

## What Cordimancy already does with labels

Four moves recur, and The Clanless can build on each:

1. **A label answered with a name.** *"I am no boy! I am chief of Kelun clan"* (`:830`), against *"boy"* (`:759`, `:829`).
2. **A label reversed by keeping it.** Malena takes the gossip's *"Hasha's crazy son"* and turns it: *"tell your friends that Hasha's crazy son rode out to do battle for those who couldn't help themselves"* (`:2894`). This is the title's inversion in miniature: keep the word and change what it means.
3. **A name given in love is not a label.** *Stonecaster* (Oji, thirteen times), *Lena* (Shivi, `:2321`), *Pak'ita* (`:4234`). And Shivi's chosen kinship: *"She said we could call her that"*, grandma (`:4727`). The difference from a label is who gives the name and whether the bearer accepts it. *Grandma* is canon's precedent for *daughter*.
4. **Self-labels are where the book's people lie to themselves.** Malena's *"There were no survivors here"* (`:1083`), *"soiled"*, *"damaged beyond repair"* (`:1055`, `:1075`), *"I killed him"* (`:4301`). Toril's *"Fails-at-Everything-that-Matters"* (`:1499`). Each is answered by someone with standing, Shivi (`:4325`) or Gitám (`:1503`), and not by the bearer.

**The title's seed.** The word *clanless* never appears in Cordimancy. *"No clan"* appears once, in narration, about Oji: *"who had no family, no clan, and no magic except the pain of an aiki trance … he was a heart, too"* (`:4745`). So the novel's last chapter already sets "no clan" beside "a heart". The Clanless's word first arrives as Rovin's sentence.

## Antagonists

- **Gorumim:** *White Hair* (Kinora, `:1187`), F by his own admission: *"I got this white hair from sata throats, not my own vows"* (`:4501`). *"I am the raja"* (`:838`), a self-label.
- **Rovin:** labels flow from him (*coward*, *crazy*, the magic charge), and *clanless* is his. He labels others and is barely labeled himself: *"too yellow"* (`:2894`), *"those vipers"* (`:1304`).
- **Veshumi:** *"six years to produce issue … nothing to show for it"* (`:225`, her father). That is close to *barren*, and the text never says whether it's true. `AGENTS.md` §6 names *barren* as exactly the kind of label a plan must not use to describe her.

## The rungs to *clanless* `[?]`

1. **The sentence.** Rovin pins *clanless* on Toril's people, meaning a sentence that ends them (`plan/truth-profiles/rovin.md`, "The label"). Tupa's walk out of the river makes the sisters clanless at the moment she takes a name (ruling, Q-M93X).
2. **The wound.** The word presses on each of them in a different way: no protection under the clan's law, no share in its harvest, no standing. The brief requires that *"the feelings of isolation need to be real."*
3. **The reversal.** Someone keeps the word and changes what it means, as Malena did with *"Hasha's crazy son"* (`:2894`). Kinora says it first (ruling, Q-J9NP). She takes the word literally, with no irony: if they are clanless and she wants to be where they are, she is clanless too. That claims a shared condition, which answers her blind spot before anyone says *daughter*. Oji is the exile who lived it first, the only character Cordimancy calls clanless (`:4745`), so he could be the one she hears it from, and the one who understands what she's done. The power is in what it costs the adults to hear a child choose exile in order to belong. The guard is the style canon's Kinora section: the narration stays inside her reasoning and doesn't comment on it.
4. **Held against cost.** After the reversal, the outside world keeps using the word as a sentence, and each use has to be held against again. The outside-model check flagged this rung (`plan/layers.md`): without it, the band calls itself clanless and then behaves like any found family.
