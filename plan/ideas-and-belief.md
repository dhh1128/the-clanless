# L16 — Ideas and belief

Planning layer L16 (`plan/layers.md`). This layer covers each idea Cordimancy taught, and each new one this book adds. For every idea it records who carries it now that the elders are dead, in which conversation, what saying it costs the speaker, the counter-voice, and whether the mythology holds it true or only believed. Daniel: this layer *"connects strongly to the whole raison d'etre of the book and … acts as commentary on its title"* (ruling, Q-XMPJ).

This is the **canon half** (ruling, Q-PR4D). Citations are to `~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md` at cordimancy commit `64b2f69`. Placement waits for the scene list.

## How Cordimancy carried its ideas

- **Elders to the young, nearly always.** Shivi taught Malena; Gitám and the priest taught Toril; Paka told the legend; Oji taught Toril about the paths. Of those teachers, Shivi and Paka are dead, Gitám doesn't speak casually, and Oji is thirteen. In The Clanless the ideas pass **between equals** (Toril and Malena, Malena and Desta, Toril and Oji), or **from the principals to the next generation** (Malena to Kinora and Tupa). That is a different kind of scene: an idea found together rather than received.
- **Through standing and a hidden cost.** Shivi's teaching lands because her own loss stands behind it, and the reader learns about Nanar only later (`:2385`, `:2804`–`2810`). The style canon's §5 resolution applies: an idea is stated by someone with standing, to one person, with a cost behind it, so it reads as an exchange and not a lecture.
- **Through story and parable.** First Man (`:1414`–`1432`), the bread and the oven (`:2529`–`2550`), Lin and Viro (`:3899`–`3991`), Shivi's basket (`:4311`–`4317`). A story persuades where an argument wouldn't.
- **Against a counter-voice.** Oji against the sata (`:2343`–`2351`), the shimsal's *"Power is the coin that buys safety"* against Toril's *"careful and kind"* (`:815`, `:837`), First Man against Gitám.
- **Desta is not Shivi come back** (`plan/truth-profiles/desta.md`). Her wisdom is common sense and plain kindness, not revelation. Keeping it practical is what stops her becoming a replacement.

## What The Clanless adds, from the brief and the rulings

- **N1. Labels lie; names are chosen and held.** The book's spine (`plan/brief.md`; `plan/labels-and-names.md`). It is carried by the band's taking of *clanless* (ruling, Q-J9NP).
- **N2. Labor and heart, received.** Malena's swept-away name, *"far more profound and desirable than she once credited"* (`plan/brief.md`). I6 is the bridge: the joy after labor.
- **N3. Love even at extremely high cost, never articulated** (`plan/brief.md`; `plan/the-band.md`). It is the one idea the book carries without anyone saying it.
- **N4. Belonging is not wages.** Paka's *"Just because I'm not much good that way, doesn't mean you don't need me"* (`:1916`), against Kinora's and Oji's blind spots (`plan/the-band.md`).
- **N5. Truth told by refusing both lies.** Daniel's essay (`~/code/me/sivanea.com/on-lying-and-truth-telling.md`) and I7. It is carried by Malena telling Toril, and by Desta's plain kindness.

## Who might carry each now `[?]`

These are proposals for the outline to test, not placements:
- **Toril and Malena**, as equals falling in love (Daniel: *"who are falling in love, it is to be hoped"*): I6 and N2 (the joy after labor, found together), I7 (forgiveness between them, not given from above), I3 (letting each other choose).
- **Malena and Desta:** N5 and N4, as common sense. Desta's *"anything can be managed"* meets I6, the grief that can't be managed.
- **Toril and Oji:** I8 (what each path gives up, now that Toril has crossed toward the sata fork, `plan/capabilities.md` §6) and N4 (Oji's blind spot).
- **Malena to Kinora and Tupa:** I1 and I3, the elders' teaching passed down a generation. Malena becomes, carefully, a teacher.
- **Gitám:** whether he speaks at all in The Clanless is open. His register is ruled (Q-ZG39).
- **Lin's legend (I12, I13)** points to book 3's redeemed Rift (`plan/sequels.md`). Lin befriending the homeless waifs and learning their names is the clanless before the word.
