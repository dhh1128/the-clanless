# L14 — Capabilities

Planning layer L14 (`plan/layers.md`). Every capability in the book: its rule, where the rule is taught, where it is exploited, and what it costs. A rule exploited before it is taught is a defect. The design follows Cretaceous's `~/code/me/cretaceous/plan/tech-rules.md`.

This is the **canon half** (ruling, Q-PR4D): what Cordimancy establishes, harvested with line citations from `~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md` at cordimancy commit `64b2f69` (`:LINE`) and the bonus prologue `~/code/me/sivanea.com/cordimancy/bonus-prologue.md` (`BP:LINE`). The allocation half, saying where each rule is taught and exploited in The Clanless, waits for the scene list. **[belief]** marks something a character believes or guesses, as distinct from what the narration states. Each *Open* line is a question The Clanless has to answer, and each answer is Daniel's.

## The governing law

*"Magic is entwined with the life force"* (`:1557`, Gitám). Every capability below trades life, years, fertility or senses for power, or draws on someone else's. Two corollaries carry most of the book:
- Unbound magic costs the kindler little (*"a flame is not spent when it kindles another"*), but healing *"goes into the heart and nerve and sinews of another, and it must come from the same place"* (`:1573`).
- Blood magic *"works by taking, not giving"* (`:3985`). Heart magic is the opposite pole, and its rules are the least stated (§12).

## 1. The naming ordeal and the three paths

- **Rule.** At the river a child embraces the magic and becomes osipi, renounces it and becomes sata, or yields in between and stays human (`:81`, `:102`, `:130`).
- **What each path gives up.** Sata lose fertility, taste and color (`:1557`); Oji adds music and color, *"only gray"* (`:2343`) [belief]. Osipi lose years (`:1557`), dying *"at twenty or thirty"* (`:643`). Humans keep both, and get whatever gift *"the Five choose"* (`:2377`).
- **Taught.** Toril's ordeal (`:38`–`131`); the *Memimir Taran-ya* verses (`:81`–`104`); Gitám's law (`:1557`–`1558`); the road debate (`:2330`–`2388`); the prologue (`BP:115`).
- **Cost and limit.** Painful (`:110`–`119`). *"The Five offer no unchoosing"* (`BP:43`); Gorumim, *"We only face the fork once"* (`:4540`). Toril's unwinding contradicts both (§6). A born osipi has no choice (`:2380`).
- **Open.** Whether The Clanless shows a naming day (Tupa's self-name is open, `plan/truth-profiles/kinora-and-tupa.md`; Kinora is eight or nine and years from hers).

## 2. Kindling and the kinds of kindler

- **Rule.** A human may leave the river with a talent focused through one sense: hand (`:347`), lip (`:349`), eye or ear (`:124`). Anyone who skips the ordeal is a heart (`:125`), thought *"a dud"* (`:125`) [belief, overturned in §12].
- **Taught.** `:123`–`125`, `:347`–`349`. *"A lip almost always trumps a hand or an eye"* (`:374`) [the Voice's claim].
- **Cost and limit.** Hand magic is slow and depleting (`:348`). Healing *"plumbed the life force of its user more than any other kind"* (`:287`).
- **Open.** Which living characters kindle. Hasha (hand) is dead; Toril is no longer a lip (§3, §6). Desta, Corim and Kael are unestablished.

## 3. Lip magic (Toril, until the ordeal)

- **Rule.** Words in the ancient tongue, shaped by mouth and throat, released by speaking or spitting (`:285`–`287`, `:365`–`366`). He needs the right words (`:349`).
- **Exploited.** *Dægezhív izíg* for Hasha's breathing (`:285`); the itch curse that wins the staff (`:365`–`374`); *Maurozh* on a sling stone (`:611`); *Bulen zhová* on the staff's brass (`:1016`); stopping bleeding (`:1282`); the last healing (`:1546`).
- **Cost and limit.** Healing leaves him trembling (`:287`, `:659`). After the ordeal: *"I'm not a lip… Not anymore"* (`:2201`). He can't light a fire (`:2161`–`2175`).
- **Open.** None of this is available to Toril in The Clanless, and at the end of Cordimancy he is *"stoneless, semi-mute"* (`plan/cast.md`). A drafter reaching for his old magic is the defect this layer exists to catch.

## 4. Voices and shimsals

- **Rule.** A Voice writes the message, burns it, rubs the ash into her ear lobes and whispers (`:312`, `:347`). Shimsals are gifted Voices who proxy continuously, sometimes relay sight, work in twin pairs (`:752`), and relay attitude (`:753`).
- **Cost and limit.** *"High price"* (`:2880`). Vows of discretion (`:753`). Noemi's Voice is dead (`:860`).
- **Open.** Whether the band can reach a Voice at all once clanless, and at what price. Nima, Sotalio's Voice in the Gemini files, is only an option (Q-XB2J).

## 5. The staff of Kelun

- **Rule.** It carries a *linger*, visible only to Toril: words of flame-colored light (`:379`–`390`). It shows *"Hold your name"* (`:390`), *"Choose another path"* (`:2568`), *"Cut across"* (`:3403`).
- **Exploited.** It points by brightening (`:3715`–`3720`); whoever holds it sees through the Rift, and Malena sees leagues off and in the dark (`:3775`–`3787`, `:3811`); it goes dark while Toril is angry (`:4219`–`4224`); it meets Gorumim's bolt and doesn't break (`:4627`, `:4713`); Gorumim brands Toril with it (`:4475`–`4482`).
- **Cost and limit.** No cost stated. Only the holder sees; signals are intermittent.
- **The magic in Malena.** Toril: *"It went into you … Whatever was taken out of me"* (`:3793`) [belief]. The narration shows her unusual vigor and healing (`:2171`, `:2394`, `:3634`) and a glimpse of magical structure at the palace (`:4604`).
- **Open.** The staff leaves in the escalated trade with Rovin, placement open (`plan/truth-profiles/toril.md`). What it shows before then, and whether Malena still sees anything through it, are Daniel's to decide.

## 6. Kavro shilmar and Toril's unwinding

- **Rule.** A burned scroll states the sacrifice; a priest channels and remembers nothing (`:1385`); the Speaker asks for a name, and *"Naming is creation"* (`:1420`). The name must be one's own (`:1495`).
- **Taught.** `:1341`–`1511`, `:1555`–`1575`, `:1716`–`1722`, `:2529`–`2550`. Toril tells Malena at `:2204`–`2234`.
- **The unwinding.** Gitám: *"the portion that I wove into your body remains. Would you covenant now to unweave it?"* (`:1574`). It powers Malena's healing (`:1546`–`1548`).
- **Cost and limit.** *"All magic has a price—the great magics most of all"* (`:1343`). The scroll buys entry only (`:1560`). The ordeal is ongoing: *"Now the ordeal begins again"* (`:1511`). The unwinding cost Toril his fertility, *"Stoneless now!"* (`:4542`), weakness (`:1609`), and sata traits: no chill in the river (`:2652`) and fading color (`:2364`, `:4579`).
- **Open.** Whether The Clanless revisits kavro shilmar, and how much of it Toril remembers (memory *"fade[s]"*, `:2208`).

## 7. Shivi's hand magic

- **Rule.** Woven through touch and humming; illusions need something close to the real thing (`:1946`).
- **Exploited.** Warmth and fever held down (`:1283`, `:1900`); chickweed made into daisies (`:1942`–`1946`); disguising herself and Malena (`:3070`–`3107`); taking Tupa's appearance and dying in her place (`:4734`).
- **Cost.** *"Completely wore me out"* (`:3078`); collapse (`:3147`–`3150`).
- **Open.** Shivi is dead. What carries forward are her teachings (L16) and the acorn (L10). Malena's realization that Shivi's power *"hadn't come from manumancy. Not really"* (`:4748`) belongs to §12.

## 8. Resonances of dead kindlers

- **Rule.** *"Ordinary folk died cleanly, but magic wielders sometimes lingered"*: an ear leaves sound, a hand leaves texture (`:965`–`966`, `:995`–`996`). Blood magic leaves something *"roiling and sinister"* (`:1884`).
- **Limit.** A weak echo fades in hours or days; it can be dissipated *"properly so its owner could be free"* (`:966`), and the procedure is never described.
- **Open.** Whether Shivi, a hand who was also a heart, left one.

## 9. Osipi senses, speed and limits

- **Rule.** *"Time ran faster through their veins"* (`:641`). They can't manipulate magic directly (`:790`).
- **Exploited.** Oji's smell, speed, sense of north, sign speech and sight past violet (`:625`, `:684`, `:2338`, `:2365`, `:2464`).
- **Cost and limit.** Short life, constant hunger and naps (`:643`); *"Endurance is not their talent"* (`:2813`); cold-intolerant (`:629`, `:2163`, `:2592`).
- **Open.** The road goes north into winter (`plan/brief.md`). Oji's cold is a load-bearing constraint for L7, and the starving osipi in the south for L13.

## 10. Aiki

- **Rule.** An osipi who masters *"the rhythm and flow of his body"* becomes *"more osipi"* for a short time (`:790`–`791`).
- **Exploited.** Oji at the ahu ambush (`:2756`), in the Rift (`:3495`), against Luim (`:4615`–`4643`).
- **Cost.** *"Erasing weeks or months of vitality from an already short life, and causing agony"* (`:792`). Afterward he is *"burnin' up"* (`:2785`) for about an hour (`:2802`) [Shivi's estimate].
- **Feeding blood.** Oji fed Cricket from his thumb *"After I walked in aiki"* (`:3370`), and Cricket's bruise and lip healed (`:3375`). No rule or cost is stated, and Malena calls it taboo (`:3371`).
- **Open.** Every aiki trance in The Clanless spends days of a life that ends in book 3 (`plan/sequels.md`). L7 counts them. Whether feeding blood has a rule is Daniel's.

## 11. Blood magic and Gorumim

- **Rule.** *"It is evil to take another's life essence to strengthen your magic"* (`:3950`); it needs *"blood that matters"* to the land (`:3962`). Gorumim *"cheated the three-fold path"* (`:2030`) and is *"quite a lip"* with *"white hair from sata throats"* (`:4501`). The prologue shows the method: osipi blood burned to ash on his lips for speed and senses (`BP:49`–`55`), sata blood smoke for cold endurance (`BP:132`–`141`).
- **Exploited.** Horn suffocation (`:1216`–`1222`); shield (`:1766`, `:2515`, `:4607`); the reaper curse on Noemi (`:1467`–`1520`, `:1755`); possession through wolforen and oreni (`:2107`–`2109`, `:1968`); the wave under the barge (`:3543`, `:4191`–`4197`); lightning (`:4621`); self-edited memory to pass the raja's loyalty gate (`:1785`, `:1791`–`1794`); the blood-of-the-Crown plan (`:1795`–`1804`, `:4490`).
- **Cost and limit.** *"It costs to work such magic. It takes time"* (`BP:155`); the victims pay (`BP:177`). Possession needs years of self-torture (`:2151`). *"Every shield has a weakness"* (`:1779`) [belief].
- **Why it failed at the palace.** The power rushed back on him (`:4636`) after he killed Shivi disguised as Tupa (`:4734`). Toril: *"Tangled with a heart … had a mind and a power of its own"* (`:4738`) [character's explanation].
- **Open.** Gorumim is alive (`plan/cast.md`) and commands armies in book 3 (`plan/sequels.md`). What he can still do after the palace, and what it costs him, is the largest open question in this layer.

## 12. Heart magic (cordimancy)

- **Rule as believed.** Hearts are duds (`:125`); *"You're a heart, like all the rest of us"* (`:4740`, her mother). A kada's tale: *"a heart kindled once… cost him everything"* (`:1916`) [legend].
- **Rule as the ending reveals it.** Malena: Paka, Oji, Toril and Shivi were all hearts (`:4744`–`4748`), *"Tangled with hearts"* (`:4749`). Malena's own intuitions about people are *"always reliable"* (`:2165`) and *"She had not been wrong about that heart"* (`:4518`). Cordimancy's foreword: *"The hero's journey, and the heart magic, is for all of us"* (`:32`).
- **Cost and limit.** Not stated beyond the legend and Shivi's death.
- **Open.** This is The Clanless's theme (`plan/brief.md`, "Heart magic over blood magic"), and it is the one capability without a stated rule. The book has to decide how much of its rule to state, and the brief's line, *"the power that matters is not the talent anyone was renowned for,"* argues for stating little and showing much. L9's crisis field tracks where it decides an outcome.

## 13. Gitám

- **Rule.** *"Your name conducts my power"* (`:1509`). *"I provide sparks, but the wood and the hammer … must come from you"* (`:1443`). The Speakers bound themselves by their own law (`:1563`). He cannot choose for a person (`:1481`).
- **Who he is.** *"God of dregs, lost causes, broken pottery, and orphans"* (`:193`), the Unpaired One who adopted anyone the other Speakers didn't want. *"One of my names is Help-of-the-helpless"* (`:1572`).
- **Open.** He is the clanless's god by definition (`plan/brief.md`). How and whether he speaks in The Clanless is L16's question; his register is ruled (Q-ZG39).

## 14. The Blood Rift, pishachas and Lin's tree

- **Rift.** Torn by Viro's blood magic (`:3899`, `:3986`–`3989`); haze, distorted voices, a spinning compass, heat, no water (`:3472`–`3600`).
- **Pishachas.** Beetle-like, many-armed, riding suvars; they track by smell (`:3660`, `:3785`, `:3794`). No magic attributed.
- **Lin's tree.** No rule. Toril senses *"an inversion of cause and effect"*: the tree made the soil and the spring (`:3889`) [intuition]. Shivi's heartstone is an acorn (`:4750`–`4751`).
- **Open.** Book 3 founds a settlement in the Rift (`plan/truth-profiles/corim.md`, the wealth scenario). Whether the tree and the acorn have a rule is open.

## 15. Other

- **Rakshasa.** Communicating with one takes *"a powerful wielder of magic"* (`:900`) [Malena]; *"The spell wears off at midnight"* (`:908`).
- **Lingers.** *"Spells housed in some specially prepared object"* (`:1214`), as in the staff (`:382`).
- **The deep magics.** The *Kaporo Tarana* says The Five permit *"only three deep magics"* (`:3948`) [Lin's citation].

*Kaporo Tarana* (`:3948`), *Dægezhív izíg* (`:285`), *Shatashík moku'opi sana ar* (`:4543`), *satarisu* (`:1557`) and *linger* (`:379`). These join the terms already waiting for Daniel (Korlia, Renir, Tarkanal, Lumira, *Bulen zhová*, the invocation at `:1390`).
