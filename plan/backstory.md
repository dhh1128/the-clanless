# Backstory register

Habits, history, memories and ordinary life for each character, one id per item, spent in scenes through TEXTURE (`~/code/me/novel-method/runbook.md` A6). The full harvest from Cordimancy has not been done yet. The items below are rulings Daniel has already made.

## The Shavir family

- **SH1. The parents of Veshumi, Malena and Tupa are dead, and they are never found.** The bandits took no adult prisoners, and they took Tupa and her pinto on the road, while she was traveling with her parents (`~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md:879`, `:1614`, `:4695`: *"The bandits caught us on the road. They only needed children."*). The parents were never seen again. All three daughters feel that they are dead. No body, scene or messenger confirms it. Tupa's memory of what she saw when she was taken can strengthen the implication.
- **SH2. In this family, childbirth kills women.** *"I put my mam in her grave, and my sister died in childbirth as well. Veshumi is still trying."* (`~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md:224`)
- **SH3. Veshumi has been married six years into Rovin's line, to cement the family's ties to him, and has no child.** Her father's words: *"Our oldest has had six years to produce issue to cement our ties to Rovin. And nothing to show for it."* (`~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md:224`)

## Oji and Luim

- **OJ1. Oji doesn't know whether he killed his father.** The canon: the duel *"broke apart. Something careened across the plaza and crashed through glass at the front of the palace; another something reached the steps and shot up"* (`~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md:4641`). The one who reached the steps is Oji, because he *"brought all five of the children from the steps"* (`:4675`). So Luim is the one who crashed through the palace's front glass just before the palace collapsed (`:4643`–`4647`), and nobody sees him again. The last thing Oji saw of his father was that, and then he turned to the children. Malena assumed Oji had *"gone down with the building"* (`:4659`). Oji's own blow sent Luim through the glass. He knows he hurled his father into a building that fell a breath later, he doesn't know whether that killed him, and he didn't look back because five children were chained on the steps.
- **OJ2. Oji has already declared his allegiance, in Cordimancy chapter 32.** At the campfire he says: *"it would have been nice to declare an allegiance, instead of knowing mine from birth"* (`~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md:2380`). Shivi answers: *"Everybody gets to decide what they'll become. You decide it every day."* (`:2381`). Later, on the trail, four ahu offer him Luim's forgiveness *"if you came with the blood of the woman on your blade"*. He looks at Malena with *"a hollow expression of loss"* (`:2731`), and then he declares: *"I have no interest in Gorumim's approval. Nor in Luim's idea of honor. … Yet to respect the friendship of this stonecaster, and his wife, and to save the children you plan to murder, I will die today with a smile on my lips. After I kill all of you."* (`:2735`). In the palace he chooses the same way a second time (OJ1). The Clanless doesn't give him a first allegiance. It shows what living by the one he declared costs him.
- **OJ3. What he hid, and what others called him.** He never told them Luim was his father, or that he walks in aiki (`:2732`, `:2790`). His ivona was empty. Ten ahu once jumped him, beat him unconscious and left him for dead (`:2717`). Malena called him an *"almost"*, then repented: *"He said he would die for his friends instead. And I… was calling him an… 'almost'…"* (`:2799`).
