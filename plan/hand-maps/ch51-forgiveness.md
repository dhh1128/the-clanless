## SPINE

**Called back across a river.** Malena slips away from the pyre to cross the river and leave them. She expects Toril and steels herself against pleading. Instead Shivi commands her back, wades out, and refuses to let her go. She tells a story against herself and refuses both lies, that Malena killed Paka and that she did no harm. Then she holds her.

Shivi's account of forgiveness is the novel's version of the claim in `~/code/me/sivanea.com/on-lying-and-truth-telling.md` that a comforting falsehood is still a lie.

## Negative space

- **Shivi's own grief** (character and narrator). Paka burned that night, and she says nothing of her own loss. The scene gives only *"Shivi's voice broke"* (`:4316`), a look away, and *"Like Paka did."* The sentence a drafter must not write: Shivi saying what she is feeling, or Malena realizing what it costs Shivi to be in the river.
- **What Toril and Oji think** (narrator). They stay on the bank and say nothing for the whole scene.
- **The source of "a dolt of a girl"** (character). Malena says *"You said it yourself"* (`:4303`), and no line in the novel has Shivi saying it. She is *"paraphrasing and exaggerating/catastrophizing, not quoting"* (ruling, Q-CR18). The nearest thing Shivi did say is *"you foolish girl"*, shouted minutes before (`:4291`).
- **Why she is crossing** (character). She never says where she is going. The only reason given is *"If you go on without me, you'll have a better chance to save them"* (`:4303`).

## OUT

- **Changed** — Malena is held, not gone. She has heard, and been made to say, that she meant no harm.
- **Worked on and did not change** — her guilt. Shivi doesn't take it away; she refuses to let it be a lie.
- **Left open** — whether Malena can forgive herself. This scene doesn't say.
- **Refused** — her leaving, by Shivi's one word: *"No."* (`:4309`)
- **Relations moved** — Malena ↔ Shivi: from fleeing her to being held by her. Malena ↔ Toril: he isn't the one who comes, and she expected him to be.

## What this scene needed that a base form has no field for

1. **The wrong rescuer.** Malena braces against Toril, and Shivi comes. The scene's turn is who arrives (`:4290`). The map needs to record whom the POV expects and who comes instead.
2. **A refrain as a structural device.** *"Shivi kept wading"* three times between Malena's confessions (`:4298`, `:4302`, `:4304`). Each confession fails to stop her. The map needs a move type for a repeated line that carries the action.
3. **A story told instead of an argument.** The longest move is a story about Shivi's own fault (`:4311`–`4317`). It persuades where argument wouldn't. The map needs to say when a character answers with a story, and what the story is doing.
4. **Truth told by refusing two lies.** Shivi rejects *"you killed someone"* and *"you didn't hurt us"* in the same breath (`:4325`). That's the essay's position on truth-telling in a character's mouth. Truth profiles say how a character tells truth; the map needs to cite which profile behavior a scene enacts.
5. **A hard word and a soft hand.** *"Shame on you for that,"* fingers demanding, then a palm laid tenderly (`:4321`). As in ch 47, the body carries what the words can't, but here it carries both halves at once.
6. **A grieving character who serves someone else's grief.** Shivi's loss is the scene's largest unsaid thing. REGARD and NEGATIVE SPACE together have to hold it, or a drafter will either drop it or make her say it.
