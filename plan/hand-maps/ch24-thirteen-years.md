## SPINE

**What a life is measured in.** Oji's age shocks Toril, and they argue about lifespans, sata wisdom and Gorumim until Oji stops on the trail and makes him look at a flower. ## What this scene needed that a base form has no field for

1. **A per-POV lexicon for a speaker, not a narrator.** The narration is Toril's, but Oji's compound verbs are in every line he speaks, and he calls Toril *"stonecaster"* three times (`:2343`, `:2347`, `:2351`). The map needs each speaker's idiolect, not only the POV's.
2. **Shown rather than argued.** The argument about lifespans has no winner until Oji stops and makes Toril look (`:2358`). The turn is a demonstration. The map needs to mark a move where the point is carried by an object or a sense and not by a line.
3. **Waiting as a move.** *"Oji waited." "Still Oji waited."* (`:2360`, `:2362`) The pressure is the silence between Toril's answers.
4. **A theme said out loud, earned.** Shivi states the book's theme (`:2381`, `:2385`) and it lands, because she is answering one person and her sorrow is hidden behind it. The map needs to record when a character is allowed to say the theme, and what keeps it from being a lecture.
5. **Two refusals to explain, back to back.** Oji's silence at `:2355` and his head-shake at `:2388` both withhold, and the reader isn't told either reason in this scene. As in ch 2, a reason withheld needs a field saying where, or whether, it's paid.
6. **A relationship moved by a gesture inside a scene about something else.** Malena's boot (`:2374`) is the whole marriage in this scene.
