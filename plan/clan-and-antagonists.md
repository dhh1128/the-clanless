# L13 — The clan and its antagonists

Planning layer L13 (`plan/layers.md`). It covers every action the clan takes against the band and why that action is logical from inside the clan; the trap each antagonist is in, and the scene where the reader sees it; the people who stay inside the clan, and the scene that makes each one *"understandable, and heart-rending"*; and Gorumim's path toward book 3.

The governing line is the brief's: *"I want bad characters to mostly be trapped in their own bad assumptions, not deeply evil."* Gorumim is the stated exception (`plan/brief.md`).

This is the **canon half** (ruling, Q-PR4D). Citations are to `~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md` at cordimancy commit `64b2f69`. Rulings already made are cited. Anything else is `[?]`. Placement waits for the scene list.

## Where the clan stands at the end of Cordimancy

- **Rovin holds Sotalio and the story.** After Noemi he told the clan the osipi did it and sent men after them, not after the children (`:1146`–`1147`). He claimed Toril used magic to force Hasha's account of the succession (`:1157`). He also said Toril *"shamed our clan at the war council by being too cowardly to fight"*, and told the men to take the staff from him (`:1159`). He is competent in a crisis: *"Rovin steps into the power vacuum and acts decisively. He organizes defenses. People accept it"* (`:1305`). Two parijans were already in his pocket (`:307`).
- **He framed the priest.** The priest went to Sotalio to shame Rovin. *"Three of Rovin's lieutenants"* testified that he whispered with osipi at dawn, and he was nearly lynched (`:3025`, `:3333`).
- **Toril is hated.** *"The clan already… hates me"* (`:4532`). Malena *"sensed that the destruction of Noemi and her own misery were connected"* to his defiance at the war council (`:1594`).
- **The war rests on a lie.** Gorumim staged the capture of Luim's band at Bakar (`:672`, `:689`) and called the council to a war against the osipi (`:770`–`797`). The osipi harvest really had failed (`:762`, `:795`), so their hunger is real.
- **The palace.** Gorumim's coup failed and the palace fell (`:4571`–`4647`). Gorumim's magic *"Lost control. Not sure why"* (`:4711`). The raja was captured by ahu and his fate isn't shown (`:4643`). Luim went through the palace glass (`:4641`), and Oji doesn't know whether he killed him (`plan/backstory.md` OJ1).

## The antagonists and their traps

- **Rovin.** Trap: *"I alone can fix this"* (Daniel, `plan/truth-profiles/rovin.md`). The evidence he trusts is that it keeps working, and his competence is real (`:1305`). His dilemma, the war or the truth, is `[?]`. He objectifies people: Kinora is property and a precedent, the staff is a prize, *clanless* is a sentence. His one line in L1 is a hardening with a revelation (ruling, Q-YDNN): the reader comes to see the trap as he goes deeper into it.
- **Veshumi's husband.** Rovin's cousin (`:1710`), protégé and lieutenant (ruling, Q-DEFS). He treats people as positions, as Rovin taught him, his wife included. He is unnamed. `[?]` He may have been one of the three lieutenants who framed the priest (`:3025`), which would put a lie told for Rovin inside Veshumi's house.
- **The stepfather's kin.** They are trapped by need, not malice (`plan/truth-profiles/kinora-and-tupa.md`, the indenture). For them, a strong nine-year-old means survival.
- **The clan as a body.** People follow Rovin because he acted while Toril was gone, and because the osipi story explains a horror they lived through. Their fear is the soil his lie grows in. One clansman whose fear the reader understands keeps this from becoming a faceless mob (ruling, Q-TNAC).
- **Gorumim** (the exception). His backstory is the bonus prologue: Moruvish, the dispossessed heir bound to the osipi (`~/code/me/sivanea.com/cordimancy/bonus-prologue.md`). *"We know enough of his backstory to understand his hatred and motives, but he's very far down the path in many ways"* (`plan/brief.md`). He is alive and commanding soldiers in book 3 (`plan/sequels.md`). What he can still do after the palace, and what it costs him, is open (`plan/capabilities.md`, §11).

## Those who stay

The brief asks that whoever chooses to stay inside the clan be *"understandable, and heart-rending because we understand her."*

- **Veshumi.** Her sisters or her place (`plan/truth-profiles/veshumi.md`). Her L1 line is a step, then a refusal (ruling, Q-YDNN): she releases Malena's share, which is the clearest test of her dilemma, and then she stays. Her heartbreak at Tupa's naming day happens at the water's edge, in public (ruling, Q-M93X).
- **Vasari** (ruling, Q-TNAC). A friend to Toril who went back to Sotalio (`:1146`, `:1820`). He is a second person who stays, and his reason could be decency rather than respectability: someone has to keep the clan honest from inside.
- **The priest** (ruling, Q-TNAC). He nearly died for telling the truth in Sotalio (`:3333`). Whether he stays, and whether he is the priest at Tupa's naming day or that is *"Rovin's priest"* (Q-M93X), is open.

## Gorumim's path

- **Canon:** the coup failed, his magic broke loose, and he lives (`:4711`; `plan/sequels.md`).
- **Book 3:** he is commanding soldiers. Oji dies defending the people he loves from those soldiers (`plan/sequels.md`).
- **Open:** whether he appears on the page in The Clanless, or is felt only through the war his lie started, Rovin's alliance with it, and the starving osipi in the south (L1, Oji's dilemma).
