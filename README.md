# The Clanless

The sequel to *Cordimancy* (`~/code/me/cordimancy/cordimancy.md`), which Daniel wrote by hand. Cordimancy ends with the survivors of the Harvest Festival massacre in the ruins of Kikal Pilar. Toril is mute, maimed, without magic and sterile. Malena may be carrying her rapist's child and has not told him. They have twenty-two orphaned children with them, and no money, food or allies.

This book is the second rung of the novel-method ladder, after Cretaceous (`~/code/me/novel-method/charter.md`, "The ladder of novels"). It asks for richer characters, a heavier ethical payload, moral complexity, nuanced relationships, a magic system and a world mythology. Daniel's definition of the moral complexity is: *"a character that genuinely wants to be good, and that is genuinely likable, but that has to wrestle with competing values in tension … not grayness of moral intent, but grayness in the choices on the menu."*

The work is at book setup, `~/code/me/novel-method/runbook.md` Part A. Read `AGENTS.md` before working here.
